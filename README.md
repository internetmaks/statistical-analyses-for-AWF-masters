Clinical & Health Sciences Statistical Analyses

📌 Overview

This repository contains end-to-end statistical analysis pipelines developed for empirical research projects in the health and sports sciences domain. The primary objective of these case studies was to transform raw, unstructured survey exports into standardized psychometric and clinical indices, perform rigorous parametric and non-parametric hypothesis testing, and generate publication-ready visualizations and APA-compliant summary tables.
1. Occupational Stress, Anxiety, and Gastrointestinal Symptoms📁 Directory: 01_occupational_stress_gastrointestinal/An empirical investigation ($N = 43$) examining the relationship between occupational stress, generalized anxiety, job hierarchy, and the severity of gastrointestinal (GI) somatic symptoms among working professionals.Key Analytical Steps:
*Reverse-scored specific PSS-10 items and computed composite scores for GAD7_Score, GSRS_Score, and Work_Stress_Score.Performed demographic segmentation and cross-tabulated regular physical activity against job seniority levels.Key Statistical Findings:Demonstrated a strong, statistically significant positive correlation between generalized anxiety (GAD-7) and gastrointestinal symptom severity (GSRS): $r = 0.585, p < 0.001$.Identified a moderate, statistically significant positive correlation between occupational stress and GI symptoms: $r = 0.430, p = 0.004$.Evaluated differences in occupational stress between employees with prior cortisol diagnostic history vs. undiagnosed individuals using Welch's t-test, and analyzed stress variance across operational, specialist, and managerial roles via One-Way ANOVA.2. Physical Activity and Low Back/Pelvic Pain During Pregnancy📁 Directory: 02_pregnancy_activity_low_back_pain/A clinical cohort study ($N = 46$) assessing the impact of weekly physical activity volume on lumbar/pelvic pain intensity and functional disability in pregnant women across all three trimesters.Key Analytical Steps:Cleaned anthropometric data (pre-pregnancy weight, current weight, height) using regex patterns to compute pre-pregnancy BMI and gestational weight gain.Engineered a continuous weekly physical activity metric (min_tydz = frequency midpoint $\times$ session duration midpoint) and segmented the cohort into three clinical groups based on WHO guidelines ($\ge 150$ min/week of moderate activity, $< 150$ min/week, and inactive).Aggregated 24 binary items to calculate the Roland-Morris Disability Questionnaire (RMDQ) score alongside VAS pain ratings.Key Statistical Findings:Assessed functional disability (RMDQ) across WHO activity tiers using the Kruskal-Wallis test ($H = 2.82, p = 0.244$) and Mann-Whitney $U$ test ($U = 159.5, p = 0.262$).Evaluated monotonic relationships between weekly activity minutes and pain/disability indices via Spearman's rank correlation ($r_s = -0.205, p = 0.400$ for VAS).Tested independence between physical activity status and the occurrence of lumbar ($\chi^2 = 0.007, p = 0.935$) and pelvic girdle pain ($\chi^2 = 0.097, p = 0.756$).
